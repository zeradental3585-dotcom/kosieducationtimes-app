About KosiEducationTimes

KosiEducationTimes covers Bihar Board results, admit cards, scholarships, and government job openings with a specific focus on the Kosi division — Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul districts — alongside the statewide information every Bihar student needs.

How we work

Every guide on this site links directly to the relevant official government portal (BSEB, BPSC, CSBC, or the scholarship portals) rather than hosting unofficial result or vacancy data ourselves. Our role is to make the official process easier to navigate, not to replace it.

Independence

KosiEducationTimes is not affiliated with the Bihar School Examination Board, BPSC, CSBC, or any Bihar government department. Always verify results, admit cards, and application deadlines on the official portals linked in each guide.
