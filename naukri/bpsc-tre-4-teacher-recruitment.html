BPSC TRE 4.0 Teacher Recruitment 2026: 32,388 Vacancies, Exam Dates & How to Apply

Updated 30 August 2026 · Sarkari Naukri

Compiled 30 August 2026 from public reporting. Vacancy counts and application windows for this recruitment have been reported inconsistently across news sites — figures between 32,388 and 46,882 are all in circulation. We give the most widely corroborated numbers below and flag where sources disagree. Confirm everything against the official BPSC notification before you act.

BPSC TRE 4.0 is the fourth round of Bihar's school teacher recruitment, and the largest single hiring opportunity available to graduates across the Kosi division this year.

The most widely reported figure is 32,388 vacancies, with the written examination scheduled for 22 to 27 September 2026 and results expected around November 2026.

Quick facts

Recruiting bodyBihar Public Service Commission (BPSC)
RecruitmentSchool Teacher Recruitment Examination 4.0 (TRE 4.0)
Reported vacancies32,388 (higher figures also circulate — see below)
LevelsPrimary (1-5), Middle (6-8), Secondary (9-10), Higher Secondary (11-12)

Official source: bpsc.bihar.gov.in. Apply only here. Every other site, including this one, is reporting about the recruitment — only BPSC can tell you the operative dates and figures.

About that vacancy number

You will see 32,388, 44,000 and 46,882 quoted confidently on different websites, sometimes in the same week. This is worth understanding rather than ignoring.

Large recruitments in Bihar are frequently notified with one figure and then revised as subject-wise and category-wise breakups are finalised, or as additional posts are added mid-process. Websites publish an early number, never update it, and it circulates for months.

32,388 is the figure carried by the largest number of recent, dated reports. Treat it as indicative. The number that governs your application is the one in the official notification PDF on the BPSC site.

Who can apply

Eligibility differs by level, and this is where most disqualifications happen — candidates apply for a level whose qualification they do not hold.

  • Primary (classes 1-5) — the teaching qualification route here is typically D.El.Ed, along with the relevant teacher eligibility test.
  • Middle (classes 6-8) — graduation plus the relevant teaching qualification.
  • Secondary (classes 9-10) — graduation with B.Ed in the relevant subject.
  • Higher Secondary (classes 11-12) — post-graduation in the subject with B.Ed.

Domicile, age limits and category relaxations apply as per Bihar government rules. Because the precise qualification wording changes between recruitment rounds, read the eligibility clause in the current notification rather than relying on what applied in TRE 3.0.

For Kosi division candidates: D.El.Ed holders from the Bihar DElEd route are a large share of applicants across Saharsa, Madhepura and Supaul. If you are waiting on a DElEd result or certificate, check the notification's cut-off date for qualification — whether you need the certificate in hand at the time of applying, or only at the time of appointment, materially changes whether you can apply this round.

Exam pattern

Reported durations for TRE 4.0:

  • PRT (primary) — 4 hours.
  • TGT, PGT and upper primary — 2 hours 30 minutes.

The paper structure across recent BPSC teacher rounds has combined a language qualifying component, a general studies component, and a subject-specific component. Marks in the qualifying section typically do not count toward the merit list but must be cleared. Check the current notification for the exact structure — this has been adjusted between rounds.

How to apply

  1. Complete OTR (One Time Registration) on the BPSC portal if you have not already. This is separate from the application itself and catches many first-time candidates out.
  2. Log in and open the TRE 4.0 application.
  3. Select your level and subject carefully — this is generally not changeable afterwards.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required certificates in the specified formats.
  5. Pay the fee online and download the final submitted form.

Application start and end dates have been reported inconsistently. Do not rely on a date you read on an aggregator site — including the dates quoted in older articles about this same recruitment. Check bpsc.bihar.gov.in directly.

What comes after TRE 4.0

The Bihar government has indicated a longer-term plan to recruit in the order of one lakh teachers across 2026-2030, at roughly 20,000 a year. If you miss this round or do not clear it, the pipeline is expected to continue — which is a reasonable argument for treating TRE 4.0 as the start of a two-to-three year preparation horizon rather than a single attempt.

Frequently asked questions

How many vacancies are there in BPSC TRE 4.0?

The most widely reported figure is 32,388, though 44,000 and 46,882 also circulate. Vacancy counts in large Bihar recruitments are frequently revised during the process. The official BPSC notification is the only authority.

When is the BPSC TRE 4.0 exam?

Reported as 22 to 27 September 2026, with results expected around November 2026.

Do I need OTR before applying?

Yes. BPSC requires One Time Registration separately from the application. Complete it early — leaving it to the last days of the window is a common reason candidates fail to submit in time.

Can I apply for more than one level?

Rules on applying across multiple levels and subjects vary by recruitment round. Check the current notification, and be careful — your selection is generally locked once submitted.

The bottom line

The exam window is late September, which leaves a short and very specific preparation runway. Confirm your eligibility against the official notification first, complete OTR immediately if you have not, and do not let an aggregator's vacancy figure or application date decide anything for you.
