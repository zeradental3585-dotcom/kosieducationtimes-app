Kosi Division Sarkari Naukri Guide: Where to Find Genuine Vacancies (Saharsa, Madhepura, Supaul)

Updated 30 August 2026 · Sarkari Naukri

This is a reference page, updated as recruitment bodies and portals change. Individual vacancies come and go weekly — always open the official site listed here rather than relying on a forwarded PDF or screenshot.

Most sarkari naukri fraud in the Kosi division works for one simple reason: candidates do not know where the real notifications are published, so they cannot tell a fake from a real one.

This page fixes that. It lists the official sources that matter for candidates in Saharsa, Madhepura and Supaul, explains how to verify any notification in under two minutes, and sets out the fraud patterns that specifically target this division.

Bookmark these. Almost every question of the form "is this vacancy real?" is answered by opening the relevant site from your own bookmark — never from a link someone sent you.

How to verify a notification in two minutes

You will be forwarded a PDF or a screenshot. Before you believe it, before you pay anyone anything, run this check:

  1. Find the advertisement number. Every genuine recruitment has one — for example "Advt. 02/2026". A notification without one is not a notification.
  2. Open the official site yourself, typing the address from the table above. Do not use the link in the message.
  3. Look for that advertisement number in the recruitment or notices section.
  4. Compare the dates and post count against what you were sent. Fakes typically inflate the post count.
  5. Check the payment route. Genuine recruitment fees are paid through the portal's own payment gateway — never to a UPI ID, a phone number, or a person.

If a notification fails any one of these, stop. It does not matter how official the letterhead looks; district-level fakes are produced with convincing headers and seals.

The recruitment calendar

Bihar recruitment is not evenly spread through the year, and knowing the rhythm helps you plan rather than react:

  • Teacher recruitment (BPSC TRE) — the largest single opportunity for graduates in the division, now running in numbered rounds with a stated multi-year pipeline.
  • Police constable (CSBC) — irregular but very large when it comes. The physical tests reward candidates who trained before the notification, not after.
  • BSSC inter and graduate level — periodic, and the most realistic route for candidates without a professional qualification.
  • Railways, SSC and banking — central recruitments that draw heavily from this division and run on their own national calendars.

What is available district by district

An honest word about "district-wise sarkari naukri": with limited exceptions, government jobs in Bihar are recruited state-wide, not district-wise. You apply to a state body and are posted where you are allotted.

What is genuinely district-specific:

  • Anganwadi sevika and sahayika posts, notified through the district's ICDS/social welfare machinery.
  • Contractual and outsourced posts in district offices, hospitals and schemes, published on the district website.
  • Employment exchange registration, which is done locally and is a prerequisite for some schemes.

Sites promising "Saharsa district sarkari naukri 2026, 5,000 posts" are almost always aggregating unrelated state vacancies to farm clicks, or fabricating outright.

For candidates in the division: register at your district employment exchange and check your district's official site directly once a week. District-level contractual notices are frequently published with short application windows and are not picked up by the large national job aggregators — which means far less competition than a state-wide exam, for the candidates who actually see them.

Fraud patterns that target this division

  • Advance fee for a "confirmed" post. No one can arrange a government job. This is the single most common fraud and it costs families lakhs.
  • Fake appointment letters on convincing letterhead, sometimes accompanied by a fake joining date and location.
  • Form-filling agents who retain originals. Never surrender original certificates. A photocopy is sufficient for any legitimate form-filling service.
  • Cloned websites with addresses close to the official ones. Type the address yourself.
  • "Guaranteed" coaching with placement. Coaching can teach; it cannot place you in a government post.

If money has already been paid, report it to the district police. Recovery is difficult, but reports are what allow these networks to be shut down — and in this division they operate across all three districts at once.

Frequently asked questions

Are there district-wise government jobs in Saharsa, Madhepura or Supaul?

Mostly no. Bihar government recruitment is largely state-wide through BPSC, BSSC, CSBC and BTSC. Genuinely district-level opportunities are mainly anganwadi posts, contractual and outsourced roles in district offices, and schemes published on the district website.

How do I check whether a vacancy notification is genuine?

Find the advertisement number, then open the recruiting body's official site by typing the address yourself and confirm the same advertisement number appears there. Never use a link from a forwarded message, and never pay a fee to a UPI ID or an individual.

Which exam gives the best chance for a graduate from the Kosi division?

For graduates, BPSC teacher recruitment is currently the largest single opportunity, with a stated multi-year hiring pipeline. BSSC graduate-level recruitment is the other main route.

Can anyone guarantee me a government job for money?

No. Every such offer is a fraud, without exception. Government posts are filled through published examinations and merit lists, and no official, agent or coaching centre can bypass that.

The bottom line

Bookmark the six official sites in the table above. Check your district site weekly for the local contractual notices that never reach the big aggregators. Verify every forwarded notification against an advertisement number before you act on it.

And treat the physical and written preparation as work you start before a notification arrives — in this division, the candidates who succeed are usually not the ones who found out first, but the ones who were already ready.
