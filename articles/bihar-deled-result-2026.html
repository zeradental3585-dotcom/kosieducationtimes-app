Bihar DElEd 2026: Result, 30,750 Seats and How Counselling Actually Works

Updated 31 August 2026 · Results

Compiled 31 August 2026 from public reporting. Counselling for the 2026-28 session is an active, multi-round process and dates move. Confirm every date and the current round's status on the official BSEB DElEd portal before travelling anywhere or paying anything.

The Bihar D.El.Ed Joint Entrance Test (JET) 2026 result was declared on 5 August 2026. Of 2,23,835 candidates, 1,65,609 qualified — a pass rate of 73.99%.

That is the easy part. The part that decides whether you actually get a seat is the counselling, and it is where most candidates lose out — not on merit, but on process.

How the 30,750 seats are divided

The reservation structure is more layered than most candidates expect, and understanding it tells you where your realistic chances lie:

  • 50% for Science-stream candidates and 50% for Arts and Commerce — a vertical split across institutions. Your stream at 10+2 therefore determines which half of the seats you are competing for.
  • 10% for Urdu-subject candidates who passed 10+2 with Urdu.
  • 5% horizontal reservation for differently abled candidates.
  • 5% horizontal reservation for eligible Bihar-resident dependants of serving, retired, disabled or ex-servicemen.

Horizontal reservation works differently from vertical. A horizontal quota is carved out within each category rather than sitting alongside it, so a candidate can benefit from both a category reservation and a horizontal one. If either applies to you, make sure the relevant certificate is ready before counselling opens — not after you see the list.

Choice filling: the step that decides your outcome

You may select between 5 and 30 institutes, in order of preference — and allotment is merit-cum-choice. This means your rank alone does not get you a seat; your rank combined with what you asked for does. Two mistakes are common and both are avoidable:

Filling too few choices. A candidate who lists five nearby institutes and misses all five gets nothing in that round, while a candidate with thirty choices almost always gets something. If you would accept a seat anywhere in the division, list everywhere in the division.

Listing in the wrong order. Preference order is respected strictly. Put the institute you most want first, not the one you think you are most likely to get — the system already handles likelihood through your rank.

The allotment and admission sequence

  1. Registration and submission of the common application form on the BSEB DElEd portal.
  2. Choice filling — 5 to 30 institutes in preference order.
  3. Selection list published. The first list for 2026-28 came out on 20 August 2026.
  4. Admission at the allotted institute within the stated window — for the first list, 20 to 24 August 2026.
  5. Subsequent rounds follow for seats that remain unfilled.

The admission window is short. Four or five days, and it requires physically reporting to the allotted institute with documents. For candidates from remote blocks of Supaul or Madhepura this is not a formality — it is a journey that has to be planned before the list appears, not after.

Why this route matters in the Kosi division

D.El.Ed is the qualification route into primary school teaching, and it feeds directly into BPSC teacher recruitment — which is currently the largest single opportunity for graduates in Bihar.

The BPSC TRE 4.0 recruitment reports 32,388 posts, with the primary level (classes 1–5) generally requiring D.El.Ed alongside the relevant teacher eligibility test. The state has indicated an intention to recruit on the order of one lakh teachers between 2026 and 2030.

One timing detail worth checking carefully. If you are applying for teacher recruitment while still completing D.El.Ed, look at whether the notification requires the qualification at the time of applying or at the time of appointment. That single clause determines whether you can sit a given round at all, and it has varied between recruitment cycles. BPSC TRE 4.0 guide →

What we could not verify

  • Dates for counselling rounds after the first. Second and subsequent rounds follow when seats remain, but we have not found a reliable published schedule. Watch bsebdeled.com.
  • Institute-wise and category-wise cut-offs. These are produced by the allotment itself and vary every round; any figure published in advance is a guess.

Avoiding fraud in DElEd admission

  • Seats are allotted by the board through merit-cum-choice. No institute can arrange a seat, and a private institute demanding a "management quota" payment for a D.El.Ed seat is outside the process.
  • Check that any institute you list is among the NCTE-approved ones. A qualification from an unapproved institute is not usable for teacher recruitment, which defeats the entire purpose.
  • Never hand over original certificates to an agent. Institutes verify at admission; agents have no role.

Frequently asked questions

Does DElEd help with teacher recruitment?

The bottom line

Qualifying the JET is the easy half. The seat is decided by how many institutes you list and in what order, and then by whether you can reach the allotted institute inside a four or five day admission window.

Plan the journey before the list is published, and list more choices than feels necessary.
