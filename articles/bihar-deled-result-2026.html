Bihar DElEd Result 2026: Download Scorecard & Counselling Process

Updated July 2026 · Results

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

The Bihar DElEd (Diploma in Elementary Education) Joint Entrance Test 2026 was conducted from 8 to 22 June 2026, with results released in July 2026 for a total of 30,750 seats across the state's DElEd institutions — a common path for Kosi division students aiming to become primary school teachers.

Official websites: bsebdeled.com and dledsecondary.biharboardonline.com. Use your registration number and password (or date of birth) to check your result.

How to check your result

  1. Visit bsebdeled.com.
  2. Find the DElEd JEE 2026 result link.
  3. Log in with your registration number and password/date of birth.
  4. View and download your scorecard.

Counselling process

Qualified candidates are allotted seats through a merit-based counselling process. You'll typically need to fill college preferences (institutes in or near Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul are valid choices if you want to study within the Kosi division), and seat allotment follows your merit rank against your preferences and category.

Kosi division tip: if staying close to home matters, list DElEd institutes within Saharsa, Madhepura, or Supaul as your top preferences during counselling — seat allotment strongly favors your listed order, so don't leave local options further down the list if they're your priority.

Documents typically needed at counselling

  • DElEd JEE scorecard printout
  • 10th and 12th marksheets
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Residence/domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs

The bottom line

With 30,750 seats available statewide, competition varies by preferred institute — check the official cutoff trends published after each counselling round on bsebdeled.com to gauge your chances realistically before finalizing your college preference order.
