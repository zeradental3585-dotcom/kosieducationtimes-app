Bihar Board Admit Card: Why Students Cannot Download It Themselves, and How to Fix Errors in Time

Updated 31 August 2026 · Guide

Compiled 31 August 2026. This is a process guide rather than a live download page. BSEB sets admit card dates separately for each examination cycle, and we are not publishing dates we cannot verify — see the note on the current cycle below. Always confirm on the official portal.

Almost every guide to the Bihar Board admit card tells students to go to the portal and download it. That is not how it works, and believing it wastes days at exactly the wrong time of year.

Students cannot download their own BSEB admit card. Admit cards are downloaded by school heads using the school's own User ID and password. The principal then prints each card, stamps and signs it, and distributes it to students.

So if your admit card has not reached you, refreshing the BSEB website will never produce it. The card is with your school. That single fact changes who you need to speak to — and in the days before an exam, it is the difference between solving the problem and panicking at a website.

How the process actually runs

  1. Registration. Your school registers you with the board; your details enter BSEB's records.
  2. Dummy registration card / dummy admit card. The board publishes a draft version specifically so errors can be caught and corrected.
  3. Correction window. Errors are corrected by the school, not by the student, and only within the stated period.
  4. Final admit card. Downloaded by the school head, printed, stamped, signed.
  5. Distribution to students.
Official portals: biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in and secondary.biharboardonline.com. These are the only official sources. No third-party site can issue, correct or replace a BSEB admit card.

The dummy admit card is the whole point

The dummy card exists for one reason: to give you a chance to find mistakes while they are still fixable. Once the final admit card is issued, correcting an error is far harder and sometimes cannot be done before the exam.

When the dummy card reaches you, check every field against your own documents — not against memory:

FieldCheck it against
Your nameYour class 10 marksheet and Aadhaar — spelling, order, initials
Father's and mother's namesThe same documents
Date of birthYour matric certificate
SubjectsWhat you actually study, including the correct elective and optional
Photograph and signatureThat they are yours, and legible
Category and genderYour certificates
Subjects are the field to check hardest. A name misspelling is embarrassing and fixable later. A wrong subject on the admit card means you sit — or cannot sit — the wrong paper, and that is not something the centre can resolve on the day. Read the subject list twice.

A name error also propagates. The name on your admit card flows into the marksheet, and a marksheet whose name does not match your Aadhaar and bank account is the single commonest cause of scholarship and DBT payment failures later. Why name mismatches break scholarship payments →

What to do if you find an error

  1. Go to your school immediately, with the dummy card and your supporting documents. Do not wait to see whether it corrects itself.
  2. Ask the head of institution to make the correction on the portal. Only the school can do this — you cannot, and no cyber café can.
  3. Get confirmation that the correction was submitted, and check the corrected version when it is available.

The correction window is short and it is the school that has to act within it. If your school office is slow, escalating early is reasonable — a week's delay here can mean the error survives onto the final admit card.

What the admit card carries

Beyond your personal details, the card carries the information you need on exam day: roll number, roll code, examination centre name and address, exam timings, and the subjects and their dates.

Check the centre address as soon as you receive the card. In this division the allotted centre can be a considerable distance from home, and candidates from rural blocks of Supaul, Madhepura and Saharsa routinely underestimate travel time on the first morning. Work out the journey once, in advance, rather than at 6am on exam day.

On exam day

  • Carry the original admit card, stamped and signed by the school head. A photocopy or a phone photograph is not a substitute.
  • Carry a photo ID as well.
  • If there is a discrepancy that was never corrected, report it to the centre superintendent before the exam begins rather than after.
  • Keep the admit card safely after the exam. It is often required later for result-related applications such as scrutiny.

What we are not publishing

We are not giving admit card dates for the upcoming cycle. BSEB's own portal has been serving outdated notices, and the secondary sources we checked disagree with one another about which examination year the current registration and card timeline belongs to.

Publishing a confident date under those conditions would be guessing, and a wrong admit card date is the kind of error that causes real harm. When the board's schedule is clear, this page will carry it. Until then, ask your school — they hold the login and they receive the board's circulars directly.

Frequently asked questions

Can students download the Bihar Board admit card themselves?

No. Admit cards are downloaded by school heads using the school's User ID and password, then printed, stamped, signed and distributed to students. If your card has not arrived, it is with your school, not on the website.

What is the dummy admit card for?

It is a draft version published so that errors can be found and corrected before the final admit card is issued. Corrections are made by the school on the portal, within a stated window — not by the student.

What should I check most carefully on the dummy card?

Your subjects. A misspelled name is fixable later, but a wrong subject means sitting or missing the wrong paper, and that cannot be resolved at the centre on exam day. Also check name spelling against your Aadhaar, since mismatches later break scholarship and DBT payments.

What do I do if there is an error on my admit card?

Take the card and your supporting documents to your school at once and ask the head of institution to submit the correction on the portal. Students and cyber cafes cannot make this correction.

What should I carry to the examination centre?

The original admit card, stamped and signed by the school head, plus a photo ID. A photocopy or phone photograph is not accepted. Check the centre address in advance, as allotted centres in this division can be a long journey from home.

The bottom line

The admit card is a school-mediated document from beginning to end. The board issues it to your school, your school corrects it, and your school hands it to you.

Which means the two useful actions are: check the dummy card properly when it appears, and take any error to the school office the same week — not to a website.
