How to Download Bihar Board Admit Card 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

Updated July 2026 · Guide

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

Bihar Board (BSEB) releases admit cards online ahead of both the Secondary (10th) and Intermediate (12th) annual examinations. Students across the Kosi division — Saharsa, Madhepura, Supaul — follow the same statewide download process through their school.

Official website: biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in. Most schools download admit cards in bulk and distribute printed copies, but the same portal allows individual lookup in most cycles.

Step-by-step download process

  1. Visit biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Find the active admit card download link for your exam (10th or 12th) and year.
  3. Enter your school code and roll number (provided by your school) or registration details as requested.
  4. Download the PDF and check every detail — name, father's/mother's name, date of birth, subject codes, exam center — immediately.
  5. Print at least two copies: one for the exam and one as a backup.

What to do if there's an error on your admit card

Report any mismatch (spelling, date of birth, subject, photo) to your school immediately — corrections have to go through your school to BSEB, and the earlier you report it, the more likely it is to be fixed before your exam date. Do not wait until exam day to raise an issue.

Kosi division tip: exam centers for Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul students are usually assigned within or near their home district, but always confirm your exact center address and travel time in advance — some centers are in a neighboring block.

What to bring on exam day

  • Printed admit card
  • A valid photo ID if your school requires it (check your specific instructions)
  • Permitted stationery only — Bihar Board exam centers are strict about restricted items

The bottom line

Admit card errors are common but fixable if reported early through your school. Download and check yours as soon as it's available rather than waiting until the days before your exam.
