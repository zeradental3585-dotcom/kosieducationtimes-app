Bihar Scholarship Schemes 2026: Complete List for SC/ST/BC/EBC and Girl Students

Updated 30 August 2026 · Scholarships

This page summarises the major Bihar student schemes as of 30 August 2026 and links to the official portal for each. Amounts, income ceilings and deadlines are revised by government notification, and out-of-date figures are copied endlessly between websites. Confirm details on the official portal before you act.

Bihar runs more student schemes than most students realise, and they are spread across at least four different portals. Many families in Saharsa, Madhepura and Supaul apply to one, never learn about the others, and leave money unclaimed.

This page lists the schemes that matter, what each one actually pays, and which portal it lives on — so you can work out in ten minutes what you are entitled to.

Benefit figures are those reported for recent cycles and are given for orientation, not as a guarantee. Sanctioned amounts depend on your course, category, institution and residential status.

1. Post Matric Scholarship — the main one

If you are SC, ST, BC or EBC and studying beyond class 10, this is the scheme you should be applying to. Applications for 2026-27 are open until 15 September 2026.

It pays in two parts: reimbursement of tuition and compulsory fees up to a course-wise cap, plus a maintenance allowance toward living costs, with hostellers receiving more than day scholars. The reported income ceiling for 2026-27 is ₹3,00,000 a year.

The one thing to get right before anything else is which portal you use — BC and EBC students apply on one, SC and ST students on another, and the application does not transfer across if you pick wrong.

2. Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana — for girl students

This is a one-time incentive rather than an ongoing scholarship, and it is the scheme most often missed by families in the division.

  • ₹25,000 reported on passing intermediate.
  • ₹50,000 reported on completing graduation.

The graduation component is applied for on medhasoft.bihar.gov.in, under the Kanya Utthan Yojana section. You register as a student, enter your 10th, 12th and graduation details along with your university and college, and upload a photo, signature, Aadhaar, all marksheets and the first page of your bank passbook.

Official portal: medhasoft.bihar.gov.in — this is the only official site for the Medhasoft-administered schemes.

Payment is by DBT into an Aadhaar-seeded account after departmental verification. As with every DBT scheme in Bihar, an account that is not Aadhaar-seeded is the single most common reason money does not arrive after approval.

Note on eligibility: the graduation component has been reported as restricted to unmarried female graduates, and the list of recognised universities matters. Check the current notification and the university list on Medhasoft before assuming your college qualifies — this is a condition that has changed between cycles.

3. Bihar Student Credit Card — and the "interest-free" myth

The BSCC is not a scholarship. It is an education loan of up to ₹4,00,000 under the state's 7 Nishchay programme, for students who have passed class 12 and secured admission to a recognised higher education institution, up to age 25.

You will see it described everywhere as an interest-free loan. That is not accurate, and it matters:

  • Reported simple interest is 4% for general students, and 1% for women, differently abled and transgender students.
  • The moratorium runs until one year after course completion or six months after employment, whichever is earlier.
  • Repayment is in up to 84 monthly instalments after the moratorium.

It is a genuinely cheap way to fund a professional course. It is still a debt, and you should treat it as one.

Applications go through the 7 Nishchay Yuva Upmission portal, after which you visit your allotted DRCC (District Registration cum Counselling Centre) in person with your documents. Every district in the division has one. The scheme helpline is 1800 3456 444.

Kosi division note: the DRCC visit is a physical, in-person step and cannot be skipped or done online. Students from rural blocks in Supaul and Madhepura routinely underestimate this — plan a full day, carry originals as well as photocopies, and go early rather than in the pre-deadline rush.

4. Pre Matric Scholarship

The pre-matric scheme covers SC, ST, BC and EBC students up to class 10. Amounts are considerably smaller than the post-matric scheme, and in practice applications are usually driven by the school rather than the student. If you are a parent, ask the head teacher directly whether your child has been enrolled for it — schools vary a great deal in how proactively they do this.

5. National Scholarship Portal schemes

Separately from the state schemes, a range of centrally-sponsored scholarships are administered through the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in. These are distinct from the Bihar state portals, run on their own timelines, and have their own income ceilings — which is a major source of the confusion around which income figure applies to which scheme.

Can you claim more than one?

This is the question we get asked most, and the answer needs care.

Post Matric Scholarship rules bar you from availing another scholarship for the same course in the same academic session — applications are rejected on this ground every year. Schemes structured as one-time incentives, and schemes structured as loans, are not the same thing as a parallel scholarship.

But the practical treatment can vary, and the consequence of getting it wrong is rejection of your main scholarship. Before you apply to two schemes covering the same period, confirm your specific combination with your institution's scholarship nodal officer or the district welfare office. Do not rely on what a cyber café operator or a YouTube video tells you.

How to avoid scholarship scams

Every scheme on this page is free to apply for. Nobody can guarantee or fast-track a scholarship, because disbursement runs on official verification, not on payment.

  • Apply only through the official portals named above.
  • A cyber café may charge its own service fee for filling a form. That is a private arrangement — there is no government fee.
  • Never share your portal password or OTP with an agent.
  • Make sure the bank account is yours. Applications filled with an operator's account details are a known fraud pattern.

District offices in the Kosi division

For certificate and verification issues, the district administration sites carry current welfare-section contacts:

  • Saharsa — saharsa.nic.in/welfare
  • Madhepura — madhepura.nic.in
  • Supaul — supaul.nic.in/contact-details

Frequently asked questions

Which Bihar scholarship should I apply for after class 12?

If you belong to SC, ST, BC or EBC and have joined a post-matric course, the Post Matric Scholarship is the main scheme. Girl students who have passed intermediate should also check Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana, which is a separate one-time incentive. Students needing to fund a professional course can look at the Bihar Student Credit Card, which is a loan rather than a scholarship.

Is the Bihar Student Credit Card really interest-free?

Not exactly. It carries simple interest — reported at 4% for general students and 1% for women, differently abled and transgender students. The way interest is treated during the moratorium is what leads to the "interest-free" description. Treat it as a low-cost loan you must repay, not a grant.

How much does Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana pay?

Reported amounts are ₹25,000 on passing intermediate and ₹50,000 on completing graduation, paid by DBT into an Aadhaar-seeded account after verification.

Can I take the Post Matric Scholarship and another scholarship together?

No — PMS rules bar availing another scholarship for the same course in the same academic session. One-time incentives and loans are treated differently, so confirm your specific combination with your institution before applying to both.

Do I have to pay anyone to apply?

No. Every scheme here is free to apply for. A cyber café may charge a service fee for filling the form, but there is no government fee, and nobody can fast-track a scholarship for money.

The bottom line

Post Matric Scholarship is the scheme most Kosi division students beyond class 10 will deal with, and its deadline — 15 September 2026 — is the nearest one on the calendar. Deal with that first.

Then spend twenty minutes checking whether the girl-student incentive or the credit card applies to someone in your household. Both are routinely left unclaimed in this division, not because families are ineligible, but because nobody told them the scheme existed.
