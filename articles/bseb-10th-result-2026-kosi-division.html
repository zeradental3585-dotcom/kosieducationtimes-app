Bihar Board 10th Result 2026: 81.79% Passed — Scrutiny, Compartment and What Comes Next

Updated 31 August 2026 · Results

Compiled 31 August 2026 from public reporting on the 2026 cycle. This page describes a result that has already been declared — it is not a live result-checking page. Dates and fees for scrutiny and compartment applications change each year; confirm the current cycle's dates on the official BSEB portal before acting.

The Bihar Board matric result 2026 was declared on 29 March 2026. Of 15,12,687 students who enrolled, 12,35,743 passed — a pass rate of 81.79%.

If you are reading this after the result, the useful question is no longer "how do I check it" but "what are my options now". This page covers those, and what students in Saharsa, Madhepura and Supaul should be doing next.

If you are unhappy with your marks: scrutiny

Scrutiny is a re-check of your answer sheet. It is not a re-evaluation of your answers — the board verifies that all questions were marked, that marks were totalled correctly, and that nothing was missed in transfer.

  • Applied for online, within a short window that in 2026 ran from 1 to 7 April.
  • ₹120 per subject. You choose which subjects to apply for.
  • Marks can go up, stay the same, or in principle be corrected downward if a totalling error worked in your favour.

Apply for scrutiny only in subjects where the mark genuinely does not match your expectation. Applying in every subject costs money for very little chance of change, and the window is too short to reconsider afterwards.

If you failed one or two subjects: compartment

The compartment eligibility rule catches people out. For the 2025 cycle it was reported that a student must have failed in no more than two subjects and scored a total of at least 150 marks. Both conditions apply together. A student who failed three subjects cannot sit the compartment exam regardless of total marks, and a student who failed two but fell short on the aggregate is also excluded. Check the exact wording in the current year's notification, because this is the condition on which the whole route turns.

The compartment application ran alongside scrutiny in the same 1–7 April window in 2026. BSEB set a target of declaring the special and compartment results by 31 May 2026 — deliberately, so that students clearing the compartment exam can still take admission in the same academic session rather than losing a year.

That timing matters more than it sounds. It is the difference between joining class 11 in the same year and waiting twelve months.

What to do after passing

For students in the Kosi division, the two things worth doing immediately after a matric pass are unrelated to the marksheet itself.

  • Get your certificates in order. The income, caste and residence certificates every scholarship and recruitment application depends on are issued at the block RTPS counter, not the district headquarters, and an income certificate is valid for only one year. How to get them →
  • Check what a matric pass already opens. Railway Group D accepts 10th pass or ITI, and SSC GD Constable accepts 10th pass — though its physical standards are the highest of any recruitment covered on this site. Sarkari naukri after 10th pass →

What to do if you did not pass

Two routes, and they are not the same:

  1. Compartment, if you meet the two-subject and aggregate conditions above. This keeps you in the same academic session.
  2. Reappearing at the next annual examination, if you do not. This costs a year, but it is a full attempt rather than a partial one.

There is no third route, and anyone offering one is not describing a process BSEB operates.

Beware of result-related fraud

  • Nobody can change a declared result. Scrutiny is a clerical re-check applied for through the board, not through a person.
  • Fake marksheet and "correction" services are common in this division. A BSEB marksheet is verifiable against the board's records; a forged one fails at the first verification and can end a candidature entirely.
  • The only fee is the board's own scrutiny fee, paid through the portal.

Frequently asked questions

When was the Bihar Board 10th result 2026 declared?

29 March 2026. Of 15,12,687 students enrolled, 12,35,743 passed, giving a pass percentage of 81.79%.

What is scrutiny and how much does it cost?

Scrutiny is a re-check of your answer sheet for marking and totalling errors, not a fresh evaluation. It is applied for online and cost ₹120 per subject in the 2026 cycle, with the application window running 1 to 7 April 2026.

Who can apply for the compartment exam?

Reported conditions require a student to have failed in no more than two subjects and to have scored at least 150 marks in total. Both conditions apply together, so failing three subjects rules out the compartment route regardless of aggregate. Confirm the exact wording in the current year's notification.

When are compartment results declared?

BSEB targeted 31 May 2026 for the special and compartment results, so that students who clear can still secure admission in the same academic session instead of losing a year.

Can anyone change or correct a declared result for a fee?

No. A declared result can only be revisited through the board's own scrutiny process. Anyone offering to alter marks or supply a corrected marksheet privately is committing fraud, and a forged marksheet fails at the first verification.

The bottom line

The 2026 matric cycle is closed. If you passed, the useful next steps are your certificates and understanding what a 10th pass already qualifies you for. If you did not, the compartment route depends on two conditions that must both hold — check them against the notification rather than against what someone tells you.
