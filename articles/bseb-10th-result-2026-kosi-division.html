Bihar Board 10th Result 2026: Guide for Kosi Division Students

Updated July 2026 · Results

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) released the Secondary (10th/matric) Annual Examination 2026 result on 28-29 March 2026, with an overall pass percentage of 81.79%. Students from Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul — the three districts of the Kosi division — check their result the same way as students anywhere else in Bihar, using their roll number and roll code.

Official result websites: results.biharboardonline.com, matricbiharboard.com, and biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in. Always use these official BSEB domains — many lookalike sites exist that carry ads or incorrect data.

How to check your result

  1. Go to the official result website (results.biharboardonline.com).
  2. Click the "BSEB 10th Result 2026" link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your roll code and roll number exactly as printed on your admit card.
  4. Submit to view your marksheet, including subject-wise marks and division.
  5. Download and save a copy — the online marksheet is provisional; your school will issue the original.

What to do if your result shows an error

If subject marks look incorrect or your name/father's name is misspelled, contact your school's head office first — they can raise a correction request with BSEB. Do not pay any third party claiming to "fix" a result faster; this is a common scam around result season.

Compartmental and scrutiny options

Students who don't clear one or two subjects can apply for the compartmental exam, and any student can apply for scrutiny (re-checking) of their answer sheet within the window BSEB announces after results — this is usually a short window, so check the official site promptly after your result is out.

Kosi division tip: school and exam center codes for Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul are listed on your admit card — keep it safe until you've downloaded your final marksheet, since some correction requests ask for the admit card details.

What's next after your 10th result

Once your result is out, next steps typically include intermediate (11th/12th) admission, which in the Kosi division usually means applying to colleges in Saharsa, Madhepura, or Supaul directly, or through Bihar's centralized OFSS (Online Facilitation System for Students) portal used for most government and many private colleges across the state.
