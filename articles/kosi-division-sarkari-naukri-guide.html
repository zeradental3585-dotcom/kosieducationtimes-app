Kosi Division Sarkari Naukri: Complete District-Wise Guide (Saharsa, Madhepura, Supaul)

Updated July 2026 · Sarkari Naukri

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

The Kosi division consists of three districts — Saharsa (the divisional headquarters), Madhepura, and Supaul — with a combined population of over 1.2 crore as of the 2011 census. Government job openings here fall into two categories: state-level recruitment (open to all Bihar residents, including Kosi division candidates) and district-level or block-level postings specific to local offices.

Start here for official, verified openings: the Kosi Division's own government portal at kosidivision.bih.nic.in, plus BPSC (bpsc.bih.nic.in) and CSBC (csbc.bih.nic.in) for the largest state-level drives.

State-level recruitment relevant to Kosi division candidates

  • BPSC Teacher Recruitment (TRE) — see our TRE 4.0 guide for the current cycle.
  • Bihar Police Constable — see our Bihar Police vacancy guide.
  • BPSC Combined Competitive Examination — the state's flagship civil services exam, open statewide.
  • Bihar SSC (BSSC) — recruits for various clerical and technical posts across state departments.

District and block-level openings

Beyond large state-level drives, individual district administrations in Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul periodically recruit for posts like Anganwadi worker/helper, contractual teachers (Niyojit Shikshak-style positions where still applicable), panchayat-level data entry operators, and health department contractual staff. These are usually announced on the respective District Magistrate office notice boards and the district's page on the Bihar government portal, not on national job aggregator sites.

How to verify any Kosi division job listing: cross-check the vacancy against the relevant official domain (bih.nic.in subdomain for the department or district) before applying or paying any fee. Job scams that mimic government recruitment are common during high-search-volume periods like results and admit card season.

Building a simple job-search routine

  1. Check bpsc.bih.nic.in and csbc.bih.nic.in weekly for state-level notifications.
  2. Check your district's page on the Bihar government portal for local/contractual postings.
  3. Keep your core documents (10th/12th/graduation marksheets, category certificate, residence proof) scanned and ready — most Bihar government applications are online-only now.

The bottom line

Kosi division candidates have access to the same major state-level drives as the rest of Bihar, plus local district/block openings that are easy to miss if you only follow national job sites. Building a habit of checking the official portals directly is the most reliable way to not miss a deadline.
