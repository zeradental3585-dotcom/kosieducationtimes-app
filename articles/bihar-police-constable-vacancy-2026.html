Bihar Police Constable Vacancy 2026: Current CSBC Recruitments & What Kosi Candidates Should Know

Updated 10 August 2026 · Sarkari Naukri

Compiled 10 August 2026 from public reporting and CSBC advertisement references. Recruitment status changes week to week. Confirm every figure and date on the official CSBC website before acting.

A lot of what is published about "Bihar Police Constable Vacancy 2026" is speculation about a large drive that has not been notified. This page separates what is actually open right now from what people are hoping for.

What is actually notified

The Central Selection Board of Constables (CSBC) is the recruiting body for constable-level posts in Bihar Police. The advertisements reported for 2026 so far are comparatively small and specialised:

01/2026Constable (Sipahi), Special Branch83Notified 29 Jan 2026; applications Feb-Mar 2026; written exam reported 24 June 2026

Official source: csbc.bihar.gov.in (also referenced as csbc.bih.nic.in). Apply only through CSBC. No agent, coaching centre or Telegram channel can get you a form, a seat or a relaxation.

What about the big constable recruitment?

Many sites are quoting an expected 22,000 or more constable posts. We are not going to repeat that as fact, because no such advertisement is confirmed.

What can be said honestly: CSBC has run very large constable drives before — a notification for 19,383 posts is a matter of record — and the general expectation of another large round is reasonable. But "reasonable expectation" and "notified vacancy" are different things, and candidates lose months preparing for a specific figure and date that was never official.

If you see a specific number and application date for a large 2026 constable drive, check whether the page cites an advertisement number and links to the CSBC notification PDF. If it does not, treat it as speculation.

Eligibility

Requirements vary by advertisement — the Special Branch and Operator posts do not carry identical conditions. As a general picture for constable-level recruitment:

  • Education — 10+2 (intermediate) from a recognised board for the posts noted above. Technical posts may require additional qualifications.
  • Age — broadly 18 to 30 years for the recent advertisement, with relaxation by category as per Bihar rules, reckoned against a cut-off date stated in the notification (1 August 2026 in the case referenced above).
  • Domicile — Bihar residence conditions apply for reservation benefits.

Read the age cut-off date carefully. Candidates near the upper limit are routinely rejected because they calculated against the application date rather than the notified cut-off.

Physical standards: PMT and PET

Selection runs through a written examination followed by the Physical Measurement Test (PMT) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET). Both are qualifying and both eliminate large numbers of otherwise strong candidates.

  • PMT measures height and, for male candidates, chest with expansion. Standards differ by gender and category.
  • PET covers running, high jump and shot put, with the qualifying marks and running distance differing between male and female candidates.

Exact standards are set in each advertisement and have been revised between rounds, so use the current notification rather than a figure from an older recruitment.

For Kosi division candidates: physical preparation is the part you can start today, with no notification and no fee. Candidates from Saharsa, Madhepura and Supaul routinely clear the written stage and then fail PET on running time, because preparation begins only after the written result. If you intend to sit the next large drive, begin running now — it is the one component that cannot be crammed.

How the selection process works

  1. Online application through CSBC, with fee payment.
  2. Admit card download.
  3. Written examination — qualifying, and used to shortlist for physical tests.
  4. PMT — height and chest measurement.
  5. PET — running, high jump, shot put.
  6. Document verification and final merit list.

Avoiding recruitment fraud

Constable recruitment attracts more fraud than any other Bihar exam, and the Kosi division sees a great deal of it.

  • No one can arrange a post, a relaxation, or advance question papers. Anyone claiming otherwise is running a scam.
  • CSBC does not collect fees through individuals, UPI numbers or agents.
  • Fake notification PDFs circulate widely on WhatsApp before real ones are issued. Check the advertisement number against the CSBC site.
  • Never hand over original certificates to a coaching centre or "form filling" agent.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Bihar Police constable vacancy open right now?

The advertisements reported for 2026 are 01/2026 (Special Branch, 83 posts) and 02/2026 (Constable Operator, 993 posts). A large general constable drive for 2026 is not confirmed, despite widely circulated figures.

Are 22,000 constable posts coming in 2026?

That figure is speculation, not a notified vacancy. CSBC has run very large drives before, so another is plausible, but no advertisement confirms it. Watch csbc.bihar.gov.in rather than aggregator sites.

What is the age limit for Bihar Police constable?

Broadly 18 to 30 years in the recent advertisement, with category relaxation as per Bihar rules, calculated against the cut-off date stated in the notification rather than the date you apply.

What happens in the PET?

Running, high jump and shot put, with different standards for male and female candidates. Along with the PMT height and chest measurement, it is qualifying, and it is where most eliminations happen.

The bottom line

Do not organise your year around an unconfirmed vacancy figure. Watch CSBC directly for the advertisement, and in the meantime train for the physical tests — that is the preparation that holds its value regardless of when the notification lands, and it is where Kosi division candidates most often fall short.
