Bihar Police Constable Vacancy 2026: What Kosi Division Candidates Need to Know

Updated July 2026 · Sarkari Naukri

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

The Bihar Police Constable recruitment for 2026 is expected to cover more than 22,000 posts, with the full official notification released through the Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC). Candidates from Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul apply through the same statewide process as the rest of Bihar.

Official website: csbc.bih.nic.in (Central Selection Board of Constable). Bookmark this for the official notification, admit card, and result — third-party "sarkari result" sites often post outdated or unofficial vacancy numbers.

Typical eligibility for Bihar Police Constable

  • Educational qualification: typically 10+2 (Intermediate) pass from a recognized board.
  • Age limit: usually 18–25 years, with relaxation for reserved categories as per state rules.
  • Physical standards: minimum height and chest measurement requirements differ for male/female and by category — check the exact notification for current figures.

Selection process

Bihar Police Constable recruitment typically follows: written examination, physical efficiency test (PET) and physical standard test (PST), followed by document verification and medical examination. The written exam is usually the first and most heavily weighted stage.

Kosi division tip: PET/PST for candidates from Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul are usually held at designated centers within the division or nearby — check your admit card carefully for the exact venue, as candidates are sometimes assigned centers outside their home district.

How to stay updated

Since the exact notification date keeps shifting, the most reliable approach is: bookmark csbc.bih.nic.in, check it directly every few weeks rather than relying only on social media forwards, and prepare your documents (10+2 marksheet, category certificate, domicile certificate) in advance so you're ready the moment applications open.

The bottom line

With 22,000+ expected posts, this is a large recruitment cycle — but exact numbers and dates are only confirmed once CSBC publishes the official notification. Treat any number you see before that as an estimate.
