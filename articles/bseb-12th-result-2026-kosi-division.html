Bihar Board 12th Result 2026: Guide for Kosi Division Students

Updated July 2026 · Results

This page explains the process and links to official sources as of July 2026. Result dates, vacancy numbers, and deadlines change — always confirm final details on the official government portal linked below before taking action.

BSEB released the Intermediate (12th) Annual Examination 2026 result on 23 March 2026 — ahead of the 10th result, as is typical for Bihar Board's release schedule. Students across all three Kosi division districts (Saharsa, Madhepura, Supaul) follow the same statewide process to check their result.

Official result websites: results.biharboardonline.com and biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in. Cross-check any other site claiming to host BSEB results against these official domains.

How to check your 12th result

  1. Visit results.biharboardonline.com.
  2. Select your stream's result link (Science, Commerce, or Arts).
  3. Enter roll code and roll number from your admit card.
  4. View and download your marksheet showing subject marks, division, and overall percentage.

After your 12th result: what Kosi division students typically do next

Depending on your stream and goals, common next steps include: applying through OFSS Bihar for undergraduate admission (many students in Saharsa, Madhepura, and Supaul apply to Bhupendra Narayan Mandal University, Madhepura, or colleges affiliated with it), appearing for national entrance exams (JEE, NEET) if you took Science, or applying for the Bihar DElEd Joint Entrance Test if you're interested in becoming a primary school teacher.

Scholarship eligibility opens up now

Passing 12th is also the trigger point for post-matric scholarship applications for SC/ST/BC/EBC students — see our Bihar Post Matric Scholarship guide for eligibility and how to apply before the deadline.

Kosi division tip: if you're applying to a college via OFSS, keep your 12th marksheet, category certificate, and residence proof ready before the portal opens — admission windows move fast and document delays are the most common reason for missed seats.

Compartmental and scrutiny

As with 10th results, BSEB offers a compartmental exam for students who didn't clear specific subjects, and a scrutiny option to have your answer sheet re-checked. Both have short application windows announced on the official site right after results — don't wait to check.
