Bihar Board 12th Result 2026: 85.19% Passed — Scrutiny, Compartment and What Comes Next

Updated 31 August 2026 · Results

Compiled 31 August 2026 from public reporting on the 2026 cycle. This page describes a result that has already been declared — it is not a live result-checking page. Scrutiny and compartment dates and fees change each year; confirm the current cycle on the official BSEB portal.

The Bihar Board Intermediate result 2026 was declared on 23 March 2026 at 1:30 pm. Of 13,17,846 candidates who appeared, 11,21,973 passed — a pass rate of 85.19%.

Girls outperformed boys: 86.23% against 83.99%.

Scrutiny and compartment

The two routes after an unsatisfactory Inter result are the same in structure as at matric level:

  • Scrutiny — a re-check of the answer sheet for marking and totalling errors, not a fresh evaluation. Applied for online, per subject, with a fee.
  • Compartment — a second attempt in a limited number of failed subjects, with eligibility conditions set in the notification.

The Inter compartment result for 2026 was released on 23 May 2026. As at matric level, the board schedules it early enough that students who clear can still take admission in the same academic session rather than losing a year.

Do not assume the matric compartment conditions apply unchanged at Inter level. The number of subjects permitted and any aggregate requirement are set separately in each notification. Read the Inter notification for the Inter rules.

What a 12th pass opens — and the deadlines attached to it

This is the part that matters most and gets the least attention. A 12th pass unlocks several things at once, and some of them are time-limited.

Money you may be entitled to

  • Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana — a reported ₹25,000 one-time incentive on passing intermediate, for girl students. This is separate from any scholarship and is routinely left unclaimed in this division. Full guide →
  • Bihar Post Matric Scholarship — for SC, ST, BC and EBC students continuing beyond class 10. The 2026-27 window closes 15 September 2026. Full guide →
  • Bihar Student Credit Card — up to ₹4,00,000 for higher education, available after class 12. It is a loan, not a grant, and it is not interest-free. What the terms actually are →

Recruitment that opens at 12th pass

Several recruitments become available that a matric pass does not reach — Bihar Police Constable, Bihar Home Guard, BSSC Inter Level and RRB NTPC among them. They differ sharply in age limits, physical standards and negative marking, and choosing the wrong one costs a year.

Get your certificates before you need them

Every one of the routes above needs the same three documents — income, caste and residence certificates — and every one of them is issued at your block RTPS counter, not the district headquarters. An income certificate is valid for one year only, which is why last year's certificate is the single commonest cause of scholarship rejection. Apply for all three in one trip while you have time, rather than in the fortnight before a deadline when the counters are at their busiest. How to apply →

Result fraud

  • A declared result cannot be changed except through the board's own scrutiny process.
  • Fake marksheets are verifiable against BSEB records and fail at the first check — usually at document verification for a job or admission, by which point the candidature is lost.
  • No agent can arrange a compartment pass, a scrutiny outcome, or a duplicate marksheet outside the board's process.
