Caste Certificate Validity in Bihar: How Many Years Is It Valid?

Updated 15 November 2026 · Guide

Position as of 15 November 2026. Where sources disagree we say so rather than picking the tidiest number, and we link to the official portal so you can confirm it yourself. Certificate rules in Bihar have changed before and will change again.

The short answer: a Bihar caste certificate has no expiry date. It is issued once and treated as valid indefinitely. But that is rarely the answer people actually need, because the scheme you are applying to sets its own rule on top of the certificate's own validity — and that rule, not the certificate, is what rejects applications. The Post Matric Scholarship portal, for instance, accepts a caste certificate only from the previous two academic sessions up to the current one.

The four certificates, and what is actually settled

Most pages on this subject give you a confident four-row table. We can give you three of those rows honestly; the fourth is genuinely disputed, and pretending otherwise would be the easy thing rather than the useful thing.

CertificateStated validityHow settled is it?
Caste certificateNo expiry — treated as lifetimeConsistent across sources
Income certificate1 year from date of issueConsistent; one outlier says 3 years
EWS certificate1 year (financial year)Consistent
Residential / domicileDisputed — see belowSources contradict each other

On the residential certificate, you will find three different answers in circulation: that it is valid for life unless your address changes, that it is valid for three years, and that a permanent residence certificate lasts indefinitely while a temporary one lasts a year. These cannot all be right. We are not going to resolve it by picking the one that reads best. Look at the issue date printed on your own certificate, and read the requirement in the notification of whatever you are applying to — that is the only answer that affects you.

The rule that actually rejects applications

Here is the part that matters more than any validity period, and that almost nothing else written on this subject tells you.

A certificate's own validity and what a particular scheme will accept are two different things. The scheme's rule is stricter, and it is the one enforced at verification. Taking the Bihar Post Matric Scholarship as the clearest example, its portal instructions state:

  • The income certificate must be issued on or after 1 April of the current year.
  • The caste certificate is accepted from the previous two academic sessions up to the current session.
  • Both must be R.O. level certificates.
  • All academic documents must be dated within the current academic year.

Read that first bullet again, because it is the single most expensive line on this page. "Valid for one year" and "issued on or after 1 April" are not the same requirement. A certificate issued in February is less than a year old in September and still fails, because the scheme wants one from the current financial year, not one that is merely under twelve months old.

Why applications fail with a certificate that looks fine

The income certificate is where almost all of this goes wrong, and the reason is that nothing on the document announces the problem. It does not grey out. It does not say "expired" anywhere. It looks exactly as official in its second year as in its first.

So a student who applied for a scholarship last year opens the same folder, uploads the same PDF, and the application fails at institutional or district verification — frequently after the correction window has shut. The document was never forged or wrong. It was simply from the previous cycle.

Before you start any scholarship form, do this one thing: open your income certificate and look at the issue date, not the contents. If it is from the previous financial year, you need a new one, and in Bihar that takes roughly 7 to 15 working days. Starting that process on the day the form closes is the most common way to lose a year of scholarship money.

How to check your own certificate

  1. Find the issue date on the certificate — it is printed on the document itself, not only in the portal record.
  2. Compare it against the scheme's rule, not against a general validity period. For Post Matric that means: was it issued on or after 1 April this year?
  3. If you are unsure the document is genuine or current, verify it by its application or certificate number on the RTPS portal rather than trusting a printed copy handed to you by an agent.
  4. If it falls short, apply for a fresh one now and assume two to three weeks, not two to three days.

The application process itself, including the MeriPehchaan login that became mandatory in 2026, is covered step by step in our RTPS Bihar guide (in Hindi).

A note on who is telling you this

Certificate validity is a question with a lot of confident, contradictory answers online, many on sites that make money from sending you to an agent. There is no fee for any of these certificates in Bihar, and no agent can make one arrive faster. Where we are unsure, as with the residential certificate above, we have said so. Apply through serviceonline.bihar.gov.in and confirm scheme rules on the scheme's own portal.

Frequently asked questions

How many years is a caste certificate valid in Bihar?

A Bihar caste certificate carries no expiry date and is treated as valid indefinitely. The practical catch is that schemes impose their own recency rules on top of that: the Bihar Post Matric Scholarship portal, for example, accepts a caste certificate only from the previous two academic sessions up to the current one. So the certificate does not expire, but a very old one can still be refused.

How long is a Bihar income certificate valid?

One year from the date of issue. This is the most consistently stated figure and the one that causes the most rejections, because an income certificate quietly goes out of date between one scholarship cycle and the next while still looking perfectly valid on paper.

Is a Bihar residential (domicile) certificate valid for life or three years?

Sources genuinely disagree. Some state it is valid for life unless your address changes, some say three years, and some distinguish a permanent residence certificate from a temporary one with a one-year life. We are not going to pick one and present it as settled. Check the issue date on your own certificate and confirm the requirement in the scheme's notification before applying.

What does the Bihar Post Matric Scholarship portal require?

Its own instructions state that the income certificate must be issued on or after 1 April of the current year, that the caste certificate is accepted from the previous two academic sessions up to the current one, and that both must be R.O. level certificates. These scheme rules matter more than the certificate's general validity.

My certificate looks fine. Why was my application rejected?

The most common reason is an income certificate from the previous financial year. Nothing on the document says it has stopped working, so students reuse last year's, and it fails at verification, often after the correction window has closed. Always check the issue date first.

In short

The caste certificate does not expire. The income certificate does, every year, and silently. The residential certificate is disputed and you should check rather than assume. And above all of them sits the scheme's own rule, which is stricter than any of these and is the one that decides whether your application survives verification.
